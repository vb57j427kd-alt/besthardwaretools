42 mm Round Cabinet Vent Hole Cover for 35 mm Cut-Out

$0.02 - $0.03 /pc
MOQ 1 pc · FOB China · Worldwide shipping

Circular vent cover that tidies a drilled hole while still letting air pass through a perforated face. The 42 mm cap presses into a 35 mm opening and sits at roughly 9 mm total height, so it stays close to flush on cabinet backs, shoe cupboards, wardrobes and appliance housings where a panel has to breathe but an open hole looks unfinished. The face is about 1 mm thick and the whole part weighs close to 3.7 g, so it adds no meaningful weight to a panel. The base is shaped with a locating tab that snaps into the cut-out, which means no screws, no drilling jig and no extra labour on the line. Supplied in white to blend with light board and painted carcasses. A practical bulk hardware item for furniture factories, kitchen manufacturers and distributors.
