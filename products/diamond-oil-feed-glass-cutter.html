Product detail

Oil-feed glass cutter with a diamond wheel and a translucent reservoir handle that keeps the cutting tip lubricated as it scores. Feeding oil to the wheel is what lets a diamond tip track a clean, continuous line on thick glass instead of skipping and leaving a ragged edge. The steel head is held in a brass knurled collar and the blue handle doubles as the oil reservoir, so the tool stays compact enough for one-handed use on a bench or on site. It scores flat glass and ceramic tile, which makes it a normal part of glass fitting, glazing repair and tile work. The diamond wheel can be replaced when it dulls. A straightforward consumable-tool line for glaziers, tile layers, hardware distributors and shops supplying the window and glass trade. For a distributor the cutter sits in a small, display-friendly pack that mixes into a glazing or tiling accessory line, so a counter can hold the tool beside its related spares and oil without opening a separate order. Glaziers score glass with a lubricated wheel because a dry cut shortens wheel life and leaves a ragged break; the oil feed keeps the score clean on thick panes, and the brass collar holds the reservoir where a plastic one cracks. On a repair visit the cutter can be carried with the reservoir already filled, so a pane is scored in place before it is lifted out of the frame. Because only the diamond wheel is a wear part, the body stays in service through a succession of wheels, which makes it an easy line for a counter to sell once and then keep supplied with spares.
