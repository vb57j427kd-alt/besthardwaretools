Product detail

Magnetic COB work light that sticks to any steel surface, clips onto a hook or stands on its base to light up engine bays, panels and dark corners. The bright COB LED panel floods a wide area with even, shadow-reduced light, while the swivel head angles the beam exactly where it is needed. The built-in USB rechargeable battery runs for hours per charge and tops up from any phone charger, power bank or vehicle port. Compact and impact-resistant, it survives drops in workshops and job sites. Ideal for mechanics, electricians, HVAC technicians and home users who need hands-free lighting in tight spaces. The strong magnet and folding hook keep the light in place while both hands work. For distributors, this is a high-demand accessory that cross-sells with cordless tools and repair kits. Factory-direct pricing supports bulk orders and private-label options. When pricing work, contractors can carry the light as a repeatable line item costed per technician or per service van, so a hands-free lighting allowance is quoted once and reused across every future bid.
