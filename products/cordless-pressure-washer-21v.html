Product detail

Cordless pressure washer built around a 21 V battery pack, so a car, a patio or a set of garden tools can be washed where there is no mains tap or power point within reach. The pump draws from a bucket or a water butt through the 5 m inlet hose, and a self-priming filter keeps grit out of the mechanism before it reaches the pump head. Delivery runs up to 8 litres a minute, with the lance taking a straight jet for blasting mud off a wheel arch or a fan spray for rinsing a whole panel, and a foam bottle threads on for detergent work. The kit arrives with the battery, a fast charger, a quick inlet fitting and two nozzle tips, which is everything needed to start. Around 2 kg with the battery fitted, so it can be carried up a ladder. Over-charge, over-discharge and over-current protection are built in.
