One Range, Four Categories

How the catalogue fits together for importers, distributors and private-label brands.

Assortment planning starts from what your customers already sell. Tell us the categories, fitting sizes or voltage you work with, and the catalogue is mapped against them - flagging the lines that share the same hinge pattern, drive size or battery pack so a single shelf serves several product families. Where a gap remains, the partner factories quote and tool a new SKU rather than pushing the nearest stock item, and the approved specification is filed against your account so a repeat order is built to the same standard.

More about the range

From workshop essentials to complete maintenance programs, our category mix helps importers build a practical hardware assortment without coordinating multiple suppliers. Compare complementary SKUs, start with a focused sample order and scale the lines that fit your market.

Four sourcing categories under one roof: Power Tools for cordless and electric work, Pneumatic Tools for compressor-powered jobs, Hand Tools for daily maintenance and repair, and Hardware for cabinet and furniture fittings - each supplied factory-direct with consistent quality and low MOQs.

For contractors, woodworkers, maintenance teams and auto-service shops, the range covers the full jobsite chain - from cabinet hardware and hand tools for finishing work to cordless power tools and compressor-fed pneumatic equipment for heavy tasks. Order across categories in one shipment to cut freight cost and keep every project on one invoice.

Inside each category the selection runs deep - stainless hinges, slides and cabinet pulls for hardware buyers, CR-V ratchets and screwdriver sets for maintenance crews, brushless cordless drills, grinders and saws for contractors, plus air nailers, staplers, spray guns and impact wrenches for workshops and auto-service centers. Every line is kept in stock for repeat orders, so you can test a single SKU first and scale up as demand grows.

Every line above moves through the same controlled supply chain: raw-material inspection on arrival, in-process checks during production and a final pre-shipment quality gate before packing. Hardware is batch-tested for finish consistency and load strength, cutting tools get hardness and edge checks, cordless tools are run-tested at rated voltage, and pneumatic tools are bench-tested at working pressure - so mixed orders from any category arrive to the same standard. CE-marked items and ISO9001-managed lines ship with test reports and compliance documents ready for customs, keeping first orders and repeat shipments equally smooth.

Choosing which category to lead with depends on your market: hardware and hand tools are fast-moving, low-risk staples for new importers, power tools carry stronger tickets for established distributors, and pneumatic lines reward buyers who already serve workshops and auto-service fleets. Most of our wholesale partners start with one category, prove sell-through with a first container, then widen the assortment - and we support each step with market-appropriate plugs and voltages for power tools, plus export cartons and barcode-ready labeling across every category.

Which category leads depends on the market you serve. Cabinet and furniture makers usually start with the hardware range - hinges, slides, handles and glass fittings - then add hand tools for installation crews. Tool wholesalers and online sellers tend to lead with cordless power tools, where brushless drills, grinders and saws carry the strongest repeat demand. Auto-service shops and industrial workshops most often begin with pneumatic tools, pairing air impact wrenches and spray guns with the compressors already on their floor. Because every category is stocked side by side, you can open with the line your customers ask for first and widen the assortment from there.

A practical way to open an account is to anchor the order on one category and fill the rest of the container with the lines that support it - hardware and hand tools for fitting crews, pneumatic equipment for workshops that already run a compressor. Because all four categories ship together, heavy metal goods can be loaded low with cartons built up above them in the same 40HQ, so freight is paid once. Every line is quoted with its specification sheet, so your own product listing and import paperwork can be prepared before the goods leave, and a one-piece-per-SKU sample order lets you check the range before committing to volume.

The four groups also differ in what a buyer compares before ordering. Hardware turns on material, finish and the size that has to fit an existing cabinet or frame; Hand Tools on steel grade, drive size and handle; Power Tools on voltage, battery platform and motor type; and Pneumatic Tools on port size and air consumption. Comparing those few figures inside each group is the quickest way to narrow a long catalogue down to the lines your market actually asks for.

Build a focused buying program by pairing high-use cabinet hardware with dependable hand tools, then add cordless and pneumatic equipment as your customers scale from repair work to production. This layered assortment helps distributors test demand with practical SKUs while keeping replenishment and supplier coordination simple.
